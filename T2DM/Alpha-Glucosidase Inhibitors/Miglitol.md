# **Miglitol**

**Drug class:** Alpha-glucosidase inhibitor

**Model:** Not applicable

## **Rationale**

Miglitol acts within the GI tract by inhibiting alpha-glucosidase enzymes. Miglitol is systemically absorbed. Its bioavailability is dose-dependent, 100% at 25 mg and 50–70% at 100 mg. However, systemic exposure has not been shown to contribute to its efficacy. Therefore, miglitol was excluded from the IndiPHARM database.
